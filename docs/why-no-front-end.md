# Why there is no front end

Most of what an in-house legal team pays a matter management system for is a set of ordinary records: requests from the business, matters, key dates, contracts, companies in the group, outside counsel invoices and CPD. The screens exist so people who do not write queries can reach those records. That is no longer a reason to rent them. Open this folder in Claude Code, ask in plain words, and it runs the query and explains the answer.

## What you gain

- **Your own questions.** A dashboard shows what the vendor chose to chart. Here the general counsel asks "which business unit cost us the most in outside counsel this year, and how fast do we triage their requests?" and gets an answer from the same records.
- **No seats.** The whole team, paralegals included, works from one database. Nothing grows with headcount.
- **Your records in a database you own.** Plain tables you can back up, query from anything and take anywhere.
- **Rules that match your team.** Triage service level, quiet-matter days, budget alert and workload limit are settings, not feature requests.

## What a screen still gives you

Be honest about the gaps before you switch:

- **A requester portal.** Xakia gives the business a place to lodge requests. Here requests come in by whatever channel you already use (email, a form, a chat message) and someone records them, or Claude does from the email.
- **Email and document integrations.** Xakia lists Microsoft 365 and Gmail integrations and document management on every plan. This base keeps a pointer to where each contract is stored, not the file itself.
- **An external counsel portal.** Firms upload invoices to Xakia directly. Here invoices are recorded from the firm's email.
- **Mobile and drag and drop.** It runs where your coding agent runs.
