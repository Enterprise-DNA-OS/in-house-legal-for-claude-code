# Compliance check

Read docs/compliance.md, then run `node scripts/legal.mjs compliance --json`. Each row has the rule, the record, the issue and the source. Report by rule: practising certificates, CPD, limitation dates, company filings. Say what the check does not prove: a recorded date is only as good as the person who entered it, and the general counsel decides. Never give legal advice on whether a rule applies; say what the records show.
